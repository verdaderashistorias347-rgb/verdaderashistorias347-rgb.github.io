What is GO Panel?

GO Panel is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, Gerson Ortiz, to manage his own social media accounts (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Threads and X).

How it works

  • The owner signs in with each platform's official login (for TikTok, Login Kit) and authorizes the app on his own account.
  • He chooses his finished news videos, writes the caption, picks the privacy level and the time, and confirms.
  • At the scheduled time, GO Panel publishes the video to his own profile using each platform's official API (for TikTok, the Content Posting API).
  • GO Panel reads the views, likes, comments, shares and followers of his own account to show them in a private dashboard on his computer.

GO Panel is not offered to the public, does not sell anything and does not access any account other than the owner's.

¿Qué es GO Panel?

GO Panel es una herramienta personal de escritorio que usa solamente su dueño, Gerson Ortiz, para manejar sus propias redes sociales (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Threads y X).

  • El dueño inicia sesión con el acceso oficial de cada red y autoriza la app en su propia cuenta.
  • Elige sus videos de noticias terminados, el texto, la privacidad y la hora, y confirma.
  • A la hora programada, GO Panel publica el video en su propio perfil con la API oficial de cada red.
  • GO Panel lee las vistas, me gusta, comentarios, compartidos y seguidores de su propia cuenta para mostrarlos en un panel privado en su computadora.

GO Panel no se ofrece al público, no vende nada y no accede a ninguna cuenta que no sea la de su dueño.
