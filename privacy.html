1. Who we are

GO Panel ("the app") is a personal tool owned and used only by Gerson Ortiz ("the owner") to publish and analyze content on his own social media accounts.

2. Data the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok (Login Kit) and other platforms, the app may access, only for the owner's own account:

  • Basic profile information: display name, avatar and account ID (user.info.basic).
  • Account statistics: follower, following, likes and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • The owner's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list).
  • Permission to upload and publish the owner's own videos to his own profile (video.upload, video.publish).

3. How the data is used

  • To publish the videos the owner schedules, at the time he chooses.
  • To show the owner's own performance statistics in a private dashboard on his computer.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to third parties, and are used only to call the official APIs of each platform.

5. Third parties

The app does not share data with any third party. Each platform (TikTok, Meta, Google/YouTube, X) processes data under its own privacy policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from his TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or the equivalent page of each platform, and can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app's data folder. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used by a single adult owner.
